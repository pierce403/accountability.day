01

The meaning

Accountability builds trust.

Open records, clear facts, and fair decisions help each new chapter begin with confidence.

January 21 following each presidential inauguration is an opportunity to review the work of the outgoing administration and carry its lessons forward. Independent review helps people understand how public power was used and strengthens the foundations for the next four years.

Accountability starts with the facts and gives everyone a fair hearing. Where evidence calls for legal action, fair proceedings help establish responsibility and uphold everyone's rights. That care gives the public good reason to trust the process.

02

The standard

Fairness in practice.
Confidence in the future.

  1. I

    Keep records open

    Preserve public records and support the people who share facts in the public interest. A clear record helps everyone learn.

  2. II

    Follow the facts

    Give independent reviewers the space to examine evidence carefully and reach well supported conclusions.

  3. III

    Take fair action

    Ground legal decisions in evidence and the law, with care for each person's actions and responsibilities.

  4. IV

    Uphold everyone's rights

    Provide counsel, an impartial court, the presumption of innocence, and a fair opportunity to be heard.

03

Principles

Values we share.

01

Equal justice

Everyone deserves the same fair treatment under law, whatever their role or background.

02

Personal responsibility

Careful review connects each finding to individual actions and evidence, giving everyone a fair assessment.

03

Peaceful progress

Independent courts and trusted public institutions help communities move forward with confidence.

04

Shared understanding

Clear, public findings help everyone learn from the record while respecting individual rights and a fair process.
