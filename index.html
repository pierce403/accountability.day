January 21 · Every four years

Power changes hands.

The day after a United States presidential inauguration, we renew a simple democratic promise: public office never places anyone beyond investigation, trial, or justice.

§
01

The meaning

Accountability is not revenge.

It is the ordinary work of a republic: preserving records, following evidence, and applying the same law to the powerful that applies to everyone else.

On January 21 following each presidential inauguration, a new administration begins its first full day. It is a natural moment to insist that alleged crimes committed by officials of the prior administration receive independent, lawful scrutiny.

No verdict is presumed. No person is condemned by affiliation. Where credible evidence supports charges, prosecutors should bring them. Where it does not, the matter should end. Justice requires both accountability and restraint.

02

The standard

Justice must be earned
by the evidence.

  1. I

    Preserve the record

    Secure public records and protect lawful whistleblowers so facts cannot be erased with a transfer of power.

  2. II

    Investigate independently

    Follow evidence without political direction, personal loyalty, or a predetermined result.

  3. III

    Charge responsibly

    Bring specific allegations only when admissible evidence and the law support prosecution.

  4. IV

    Guarantee due process

    Provide counsel, an impartial court, the presumption of innocence, and a fair opportunity to answer every charge.

03

Principles

The line we hold.

01

Equal law

Title, wealth, party, and proximity to power neither shield nor condemn.

02

Individual proof

Responsibility belongs to people for their own acts—not to parties, voters, families, or groups.

03

Peaceful process

Accountability belongs in courts and lawful institutions, never in threats, mobs, or political retaliation.

04

Public truth

Findings should be documented and disclosed to the fullest extent consistent with law and a fair trial.

“No office is a license.
No transition is an amnesty.”
