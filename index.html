The meaning

About Accountability Day

Public officials should answer for their actions through a fair legal process.

Accountability Day takes place on January 21 every four years, the day after the U.S. presidential inauguration. It calls for independent review of the outgoing administration's conduct and legal action where the evidence supports it.

Each case should receive a fair hearing in an independent court. Public records and published findings help people understand the decisions made by their government.

02

The standard

How accountability works

  1. I

    Preserve public records

    Keep official records available for independent review and public access where the law allows.

  2. II

    Review the evidence

    Independent investigators should examine official conduct and document their findings.

  3. III

    Apply the law

    Prosecutors should bring cases supported by admissible evidence and the law.

  4. IV

    Protect due process

    Everyone is entitled to counsel, an impartial court, the presumption of innocence, and a fair hearing.

03

Principles

Equal treatment under law

01

Equal treatment

Public officials should be held to the same legal standards as everyone else.

02

Individual responsibility

Each case should focus on a person's own actions and the evidence.

03

Independent courts

Courts should decide cases independently and give each person a fair hearing.

04

Public findings

Publish findings where the law allows and respect each person's rights.
